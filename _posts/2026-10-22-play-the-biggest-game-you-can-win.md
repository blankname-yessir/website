title: "Play the biggest game you can win"
---
Winning is non-linear. If you win, you win big. First place isn't a marginal step above second. It's exponential. The difference between first and second is a world apart, a lifetime. It's everything. The best painter you know will have all the external success. Second will have nothing.

So if you want to win, create a small enough market and game where you can.

Skill is in proportion to the level at which you can win. The lower your skill level, the smaller the game you should play. Play the biggest game you can win. If you're a level 60, don't fight a level 112. Fight a level 59.

In a video game, this makes sense. Why wouldn't the same rules apply?

From fighting a level 59, you get better. All of a sudden you're a level 61. Great. Keep going and become the best. When it's time, and you have all the rewards from this level, then and only then do you move forward.

You continue to do this. You keep winning your little world while growing the skills to either own your world, or level up and win the next game.
