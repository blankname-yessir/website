title: "The hardest part of being a great entrepreneur"
---
The hardest part of being a great entrepreneur is being a great entrepreneur.

To create great external results, you need to do something for a prolonged period of time. And to do that, you can't create something out of nothing every day.

You create, then you integrate. If you only create, you create nothing. You're just a dog chasing its tail.
